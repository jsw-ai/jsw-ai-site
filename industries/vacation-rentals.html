How we help

Built from our core services, tailored to short-term rental hosts and property managers.

Guest messaging

Automated, on-brand replies to booking questions, check-in instructions, early check-in requests and house-rule reminders on Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and direct booking channels. Tricky or sensitive messages are routed to you.

Listing optimization

Rewritten titles, descriptions and amenity lists, better photo ordering and seasonal updates, so each listing clearly sells what makes the stay special.

Dynamic pricing

Pricing rules and dashboards that respond to seasonality, weekends, local events, lead time and open-date gaps, set up with or alongside the pricing tools you already use. You stay in control of floors and ceilings.

Review replies

Drafted, personal responses to every guest review, thanking happy guests and handling critical feedback calmly and professionally.

Ops automation

Turnover and cleaning schedules, maintenance tickets, inventory reminders and owner reports triggered automatically by bookings and check-outs.

Content & video production

Property walk-through videos and short-form content for social and your direct-booking site.

Going deeper

Guest messaging, in detail

We build a library of approved replies for your properties (Wi-Fi, parking, early check-in, luggage drop, pets, quiet hours) and connect it to your channel or property-management tool where possible. The assistant answers routine questions fast and escalates anything about refunds, damages or safety to you.

Listing optimization & pricing, in detail

We audit each listing's copy, photos and amenities, then update them for clarity and search. On pricing, we help set rules for weekends, holidays, minimum stays and last-minute gaps, and give you a dashboard to see the effect on occupancy. You approve every rule.

Reviews & operations, in detail

Review replies are drafted in your voice for you to approve or auto-post. Behind the scenes, bookings drive cleaning schedules, supply reorders and maintenance tickets so nothing slips between turnovers.

Example workflow

An illustrative example of how the pieces can fit together. Every setup is customized.

  1. A guest books a stay on Airbnb.
  2. An automated welcome message confirms details; check-in instructions follow at the right time before arrival.
  3. The checkout triggers a cleaning task for your turnover team, with a checklist.
  4. After the stay, a review reminder goes out, and a drafted reply is ready when the review comes in.
  5. Open dates in the coming weeks are flagged so pricing rules can adjust.
  6. A weekly summary shows occupancy, messages handled and open tasks.
